## Chapter 905: 5S and Visual Management on the Shop Floor

### The engineering question

*The area was organized last month. Today there's a box on the floor again, a duplicate tool on
the bench, a red tag nobody removed. What broke?*

Nothing broke — 5S never reached the fifth S. Sort, Set in Order, and Shine are an event: you can
do them in an afternoon and show before/after photos. Standardize and Sustain are a discipline:
they require someone to come back every single day. Most 5S programs that "don't work" didn't fail
at organizing — they failed at turning organization into a habit.

## 2 · Where the discipline drops first

A typical 5S audit, by pillar:

```plotly
5s-radar
```

The pattern repeats in almost every real audit: the first three pillars score well — they're
visible, photogenic, easy to sell in a presentation. **Sustain** is systematically the lowest,
because it's the only one that doesn't produce an immediate visual result — it produces the
*absence* of regression, which nobody photographs.

### Why 5S fails (the three most common causes)

| Cause | What happens | The symptom |
|---|---|---|
| **Stopping at Shine** | Sort, Set, and Shine are done; Standardize and Sustain aren't. | The chaos comes back in two to four weeks. |
| **Confusing it with aesthetics** | 5S treated as "making it look nice," not as eliminating waste and improving flow. | A pretty area, but no productivity gain — and management loses interest. |
| **No visual standard** | No shadow board, tag, or color code. | Everyone organizes their own way; the "order" doesn't survive a shift change. |

> **The truth about 5S.** An annual "cleaning day" isn't a system — it's an event that the mess
> absorbs and gives back in two weeks. 5S isn't a spring cleaning. It's a daily discipline built
> into how the work gets done.

## 3 · What a 5S audit finds, in practice

```plotly
5s-pareto
```

The Pareto pattern here is almost always the same: "item out of place" and "accumulated dirt"
combined account for more than half the findings — the two cheapest problems to fix, and the
easiest to prevent with a simple visual standard.

## 4 · Floor color coding — the silent supervisor

Floor marking is visual management in its cheapest form: anyone, even without training,
recognizes the meaning in seconds.

| Color / pattern | Typical meaning |
|---|---|
| **Yellow** | Aisles, traffic lanes, general work zones |
| **Blue** | Raw material staging / received material |
| **Green** | Finished product / safe area |
| **Orange** | Product held for inspection |
| **Red** | Defect, scrap, containment area |
| **Black** | Work in progress (WIP) |
| **White** | Tool and equipment storage |
| **Black + yellow (hatched)** | Danger zone — keep clear |
| **Red + white (hatched)** | Keep clear for safety/emergency access |

## 5 · Visual management beyond the floor: the tools that sustain Sustain

| Tool | What it solves | Typical frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Job Element Sheet (JES)** | Step-by-step for the station, with a photo and the "why" behind each key point — not just the "what." | Consulted at every operator change |
| **Hour-by-hour board** | Planned vs. actual per hour, cause of the gap, and action taken — exposes the loss in the same shift it happens. | Every hour |
| **Kamishibai board** | Green/red card per audit item, per day of the week. A missing card is already a sign of a skipped audit. | Daily, by sampling |
| **Obeya room** | Physical room with vision, strategy, today's SQDC, problem backlog, and actions — all in one place. | Weekly/monthly cadence |
| **One Point Lesson (OPL)** | One lesson, one sheet, with a "wrong" photo and a "right" photo side by side. | Published for every relevant deviation |

## 6 · Scoring a 5S audit

```py-r
--- python
import numpy as np

# 5S audit: 5 pillars, 4 questions each, 0-5 scale
pilares = ["Sort", "Set in Order", "Shine", "Standardize", "Sustain"]
notas = np.array([
    [4, 4, 5, 3],   # Sort
    [5, 4, 4, 4],   # Set in Order
    [4, 3, 4, 4],   # Shine
    [4, 4, 3, 5],   # Standardize
    [2, 3, 3, 2],   # Sustain
])

media_pilar = notas.mean(axis=1) / 5 * 100     # as % of the maximum
score_geral = notas.mean() / 5 * 100

for p, m in zip(pilares, media_pilar):
    status = "OK" if m >= 80 else ("WATCH" if m >= 60 else "CRITICAL")
    print(f"{p:<14s} {m:5.1f}%   [{status}]")
print(f"\nOverall 5S score: {score_geral:.1f}%")

pior = pilares[np.argmin(media_pilar)]
print(f"Weakest pillar: {pior} — the natural candidate for an action plan next cycle.")
--- r
pilares <- c("Sort", "Set in Order", "Shine", "Standardize", "Sustain")
notas <- matrix(c(
  4,4,5,3,
  5,4,4,4,
  4,3,4,4,
  4,4,3,5,
  2,3,3,2
), nrow = 5, byrow = TRUE)

media_pilar <- rowMeans(notas) / 5 * 100
score_geral <- mean(notas) / 5 * 100

for (i in seq_along(pilares)) {
  status <- if (media_pilar[i] >= 80) "OK" else if (media_pilar[i] >= 60) "WATCH" else "CRITICAL"
  cat(sprintf("%-14s %5.1f%%   [%s]\n", pilares[i], media_pilar[i], status))
}
cat(sprintf("\nOverall 5S score: %.1f%%\n", score_geral))
cat(sprintf("Weakest pillar: %s\n", pilares[which.min(media_pilar)]))
--- excel
' Scores in B2:E6 (5 pillars x 4 questions, 0-5 scale); pillar labels in A2:A6

Pillar Average (%)   =AVERAGE(B2:E2)/5*100        ' drag down for each row
Overall Score (%)     =AVERAGE(B2:E6)/5*100

Status                =IF(F2>=80,"OK",IF(F2>=60,"WATCH","CRITICAL"))

Weakest Pillar        =INDEX(A2:A6,MATCH(MIN(F2:F6),F2:F6,0))
```

### Common mistakes

1. **Doing Sort/Set/Shine and calling it "5S implemented."** Without Standardize and Sustain,
   it's cleaning — not a system.
2. **Auditing with no consequence.** An audit that doesn't generate an action plan with an owner
   and a deadline is bureaucracy, not visual management.
3. **Inconsistent floor marking between areas.** If the color code changes from department to
   department, it stops being universal — and stops working.
4. **Treating 5S as a one-time project, not a routine.** "We did 5S in March" is the phrase that
   precedes "why is it a mess again in May."
5. **Ignoring Sustain in management indicators.** If the quality dashboard only shows Sort/Set/
   Shine, nobody will notice when Sustain is slipping — until the area is back to chaos.
