## Chapter 31: Lean and Lean Six Sigma in Current Practice

### Framing

Lean and Lean Six Sigma remain **management and improvement methods**, not certification standards. The relevant trend isn't replacing DMAIC with a new methodology, but integrating continuous improvement with **reliable data, risk management, automation, AI, sustainability, and change management**.

ASQ maintains Quality 4.0 as an application field in which classic quality disciplines are combined with AI, machine learning, IIoT, big data, and automation. The most recent discussion has already started using the term *Quality 5.0* to emphasize ethics, empathy, and human involvement; in this manual, the term is treated as a trend, not a normative standard or a replacement method for Lean Six Sigma.

### What Remains Central

> **Problem → process → data → cause → solution → control → learning.**

Technology doesn't eliminate this sequence. A dashboard doesn't replace the metric's operational definition; an AI model doesn't replace validating the measurement system; and automation with no work standard can simply automate waste.

### Lean + Six Sigma + Quality Analytics

| Need | Lean | Six Sigma | Analytics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reduce waste and time | Strong | Complementary | Measure/monitor |
| Reduce variability | Complementary | Strong | Model/diagnose |
| Understand causes | Process/Gemba | Statistics | Structured and unstructured data |
| Predict failures | Complementary | DOE/reliability | ML/time series |
| Sustain the result | Standard Work/daily management | Control | Monitoring and alerts |

### The Golden Rule

**Don't digitize a bad process definition. Don't automate a bad measurement. Don't do AI before fixing data quality.**
