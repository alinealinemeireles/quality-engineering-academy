## Chapter 92: ISO 19011:2026 — Management System Auditing

### ISO 19011:2026 — What It Is and Isn't

ISO 19011:2026 is a **guide** for auditing management systems — principles, program, conduct, competence, and evaluation of auditors — not a certifiable standard. System certification and body accreditation follow their own rules; ISO 19011 should therefore never be presented as authorization to certify organizations.

A central topic of ISO 19011:2026 is **remote auditing**: the standard incorporates the guidance of ISO/IEC TS 17012:2024, defines a **remote auditing method** (audit activities conducted from any place other than the auditee's location, 3.4), and recognizes **virtual locations** — online environments where the organization executes processes regardless of physical location. The seven principles of auditing are: integrity, fair presentation, due professional care, confidentiality, independence, evidence-based approach, and risk-based approach. Remote auditing is only appropriate when method, information security, access to evidence, competence, and risk allow for sufficient evidence.

### A Process-, Risk-, and Evidence-Based Audit Method

1. Define objective, scope, criteria, team, independence, and confidentiality.
2. Plan risk-driven sampling: changes, performance, complaints, special characteristics, suppliers, and prior findings.
3. Follow process trails (input → activity → output → customer) and triangulate interview, observation, record, and data.
4. Write up the finding with requirement/criterion, verifiable evidence, observed condition, and impact — without prescribing a solution when the auditor's role doesn't allow it.
5. Assess the action plan and its effectiveness in proportion to risk; close only with evidence of implementation and effectiveness.

**Common pitfall:** a checklist is a memory aid; it doesn't replace professional judgment, well-founded sampling, or objective evidence.

### Competence and the Audit Program

Competence combines ethical behavior, knowledge of audit principles, the applicable standard, process/technology/sector, legal requirements, and the ability to communicate conclusions. The program should record risks, objectives, methods (on-site/remote/hybrid), resources, competence, results, and improvement of the program itself.

Status source: [ISO 19011:2026](https://www.iso.org/standard/19011). For IATF, regulated, or accreditation audits, supplement with the applicable sector-specific rules.

### Remote Auditing and Virtual Locations

ISO 19011:2026 gives guidance on remote auditing methods, aligned with ISO/IEC TS 17012, and its Annex A covers remote methods and virtual locations. In practice:

| Where | What the auditor should apply |
|---|---|
| Table A.1 | Methods organized along two dimensions: auditor location (on-site/remote) × human interaction (with/without). Remotely, work is observed with a *remote guide* or through surveillance means, respecting legal and social requirements |
| A.1 | At program level, the use of on-site and remote methods should be **suitable and balanced**; the feasibility of remote methods depends on the risk to the audit objectives, the level of confidence between the parties, and regulatory requirements |
| A.16 | **Joint** responsibility of auditee and auditors for effectiveness; prior technical checks, agreed access protocols, contingency plans (including extra time); ask permission before screenshots or recordings; privacy during breaks |
| A.17 g) | In virtual interviews, non-verbal communication is limited — the focus shifts to the type of question that obtains objective evidence |
| 3.6 and 6.3.2 | The audit scope and audit plan describe **physical and virtual** locations and the availability of information and communication technologies |

The instructional flow should remain: audit program → planning → execution → evidence → conclusions → reporting → follow-up → program improvement. ISO 19011 provides **guidance**, not system certification requirements; for third-party certification audits, the requirements are in ISO/IEC 17021-1.
