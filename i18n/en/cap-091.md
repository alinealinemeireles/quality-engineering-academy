## Chapter 91: ISO 9001 in 2026

### The Correct State of ISO 9001 as of August 18, 2026

As of this date, the certifiable published reference remains **ISO 9001:2015**, applied together with **ISO 9001:2015/Amd 1:2024** (climate change considerations). The new ISO 9001:2026 edition is in the process of being published and is expected in September 2026; it shouldn't be taught as an effective requirement before its official publication and before accreditation/certification bodies establish transition rules.

Practitioners should always keep these separate: published-standard requirements; certification-body interpretation; customer-specific requirements; and internal good practice. This prevents an expectation or draft revision from being mistaken for an auditable obligation.

### Application Roadmap by Clause

| ISO 9001 Block | Engineering Question | Typical Objective Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 4. Context | Which interested parties, scope, and processes influence QMS outcomes? | process map, scope, relevant needs, external/internal risks |
| 5. Leadership | Who's accountable for the system, the customer, and the policy? | objectives, responsibilities, management review, decisions |
| 6. Planning | How are risks/opportunities and objectives addressed? | risk register, measurable targets, change plan |
| 7. Support | Are people, competence, communication, knowledge, and documented information adequate? | competence matrix, calibration, controlled documents |
| 8. Operation | How are requirements, design, suppliers, production, and release controlled? | contract, FMEA/control where applicable, traceability, inspection/release |
| 9. Evaluation | Is performance monitored and audited? | KPI, satisfaction, audit, data analysis, management review |
| 10. Improvement | Do nonconformities lead to effective action? | containment, root cause, CAPA, effectiveness verification |

The 2024 climate requirement adds the need to determine whether climate change is a relevant topic to the organization's context and to consider interested parties' requirements related to it; it doesn't create a universal list of environmental controls within ISO 9001.

### Applied Exercise: From Nonconformity to Effectiveness

For a customer complaint about a wrong product delivery, produce: (a) containment and traceability of the lots; (b) root-cause analysis that tests hypotheses rather than blaming the operator; (c) correction, corrective action, and owner; (d) an effectiveness metric over a defined period; and (e) an update to risk, training, work instructions, the identification system, and auditing. Tie the evidence back to clauses 8, 9, and 10, without confusing immediate correction with systemic corrective action.

Status sources: [ISO 9001:2015](https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html) and [ISO 9001:2015/Amd 1:2024](https://www.iso.org/standard/88431.html).

### Update 8/20/2026 — ISO 9001

The correct situation as of this date is: **ISO 9001:2015 remains the published, effective edition; ISO 9001:2026 (Edition 6) is under publication, with publication expected in September 2026**. ISO itself indicates the revision will replace ISO 9001:2015 after publication, and there will be a transition period.

**Important:** as long as the 2026 edition isn't published, this manual shouldn't present clause numbers or requirements from the future edition as officially effective requirements. This chapter, therefore, separates what's already a requirement under ISO 9001:2015 from what's an expectation for the upcoming update.
