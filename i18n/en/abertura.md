# Handbook of Quality Engineering, Lean Six Sigma and Quality Analytics

**4th Edition — 2026 · Excel · Minitab · Python · R · Power BI · SQL**

Integrated technical preparation for **Quality Engineer (ASQ CQE)** and **Six Sigma Black Belt (ASQ
CSSBB)**, with an additional layer of **Lean, data engineering, and analytics applied to
quality**. The certification-prep core follows the public 2022 *Bodies of Knowledge* of the
CQE and CSSBB; standards, tools, and analytics appear as complementary professional
competencies.

> **Not official material**, approved or endorsed by ASQ, ISO, IATF, or AIAG, and it does
> not guarantee passing any certification exam. The question bank
> (119 questions, Part XV) is **entirely original** — it does not reproduce items published by ASQ.

## ASQ Certification — exam reference

> The term "Lean Six Sigma" describes the integrated approach used in this handbook; ASQ does not
> issue a credential by that name. CQE and CSSBB are distinct credentials, each with its own
> *Body of Knowledge*.

| | CQE | CSSBB |
|---|---|---|
| **Body of Knowledge** | 2022, 7 domains | 2022, 9 domains |
| **Exam** | 175 questions (160 scored), 5h18, open book | 165 questions (150 scored), 4h18, open book |
| **This handbook's original bank** | 60 questions, 7 domains | 59 questions, 9 domains |

Each question in the bank (Part XV) indicates the BoK domain and the chapter where the topic is
developed. Always check the exam's current conditions at the official ASQ source before
registering — the number of questions, duration, and eligibility change periodically.

**Three levels of statistical mastery** organize the handbook's progression:

<div class="kpi-row">
<div class="kpi ok"><div class="kpi-k">Level 1 · Engineer</div><div class="kpi-v">Fundamentals</div><div class="kpi-s">basic metrics, choosing the right test, charts and SPC</div></div>
<div class="kpi warn"><div class="kpi-k">Level 2 · Advanced Quality</div><div class="kpi-v">Application</div><div class="kpi-s">study design, ANOVA, DOE, capability, MSA, sampling, regression</div></div>
<div class="kpi bad"><div class="kpi-k">Level 3 · Data Scientist</div><div class="kpi-v">Modeling</div><div class="kpi-s">causal inference, bootstrap, GLM, mixed models, time series, Bayes, uncertainty</div></div>

Pedagogical rule: don't jump to machine learning before mastering study design,
measurement quality, sampling, and inference — a sophisticated model on biased data
is still a bad analysis.
