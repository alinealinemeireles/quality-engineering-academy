## Chapter 132: About This Handbook: Methodology and Sources

### A note on the CMQ/OE Handbook file

The file submitted as *The Certified Manager of Quality/Organizational Excellence Handbook* (Russell T. Westcott, 3rd ed.) contained only 88 pages — introductory material and the official CMQ/OE Body of Knowledge, not the book's full chapters (which run to hundreds of pages in the printed edition). Because of that, it wasn't used as a source for extensive content; the topics that overlap with the CMQ/OE BoK — leadership, strategic planning, the quality system, supplier management — are already covered in this handbook by the CQE handbooks (Units 13 and 14), which treat those subjects in equal or greater depth.

### A note on the Yellow Belt and Green Belt handbooks

*The Certified Six Sigma Yellow Belt Handbook* (Govind Ramu) and *The Certified Six Sigma Green Belt Handbook* (Munro, Ramu, and Zrymiak) did not generate dedicated chapters in this expansion because their content — Six Sigma and Lean fundamentals, DMAIC, basic and intermediate statistics, MSA, analysis tools, control — is already covered in equal or greater depth in Units 1 through 11 of this handbook, built before this expansion. Adding new chapters from those two books would have been pure repetition.
